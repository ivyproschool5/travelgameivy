# Himachal Road Rally

A family car game for the Delhi to Himachal trip. One phone, held by the front passenger, runs five mini-games (Spot It, Quick Quiz, Antakshari, Atlas Chain, 20 Questions) with levels for each player, read-aloud and voice commands, and a scoreboard that keeps day and trip totals.

Play it at https://ivyproschool5.github.io/travelgameivy/

- Open it once with signal; after that it loads without network.
- Voice commands (tap the yellow mic) need mobile signal. Talking and buttons work offline.
- Scores are saved only on the phone that runs the game. Use Players & settings > Backup and restore to keep a copy.
- AgrawalKatan (katan.html): a settlers-style board game for 3 to 4 players on an iPad, with development cards, Longest Road, Largest Army and player trades. Fan-made family project, not affiliated with Catan GmbH.
